## Is an outdoor shower a good idea?
keyword: outdoor shower

Yes, if you often come in from the beach, the pool or the yard: an **outdoor shower**, like a Fontealta column by the pool, keeps sand, grass and chlorine outside the house, and on hot days it is the simplest way to cool off. It works best on the path people already take, between the water and the door. Plan three things from the start: the water supply, a drain and a way to protect it in winter. Fontealta makes outdoor showers mostly in AISI 316 stainless steel. The [five questions to ask before you specify one](/blog/cinque-domande-doccia-da-esterno) help you plan it.

## Do you need a permit to install an outdoor shower?
keyword: outdoor shower

It depends on where you live: for an **outdoor shower**, Fontealta recommends that you check your local building codes before you build, because the rules on drainage and outdoor plumbing vary from place to place. On the product side, a Fontealta column connects to the house plumbing like any other fixture, and the drain is planned with your plumber: a catch basin tied to the house system, a gravel bed or dry well, or the ground, where allowed. Columns in the [Club collection](/collezioni/club) have a concealed body that goes in during construction, together with the drain.

## What is the best outdoor shower?
keyword: outdoor shower

The best **outdoor shower** is the one built for where it stands, and Fontealta suggests judging it on four points, not on brand rankings. First, the material: AISI 316 stainless steel contains molybdenum and resists salt and pool chlorine better than 304. Second, the finish: near the ocean, Fontealta recommends polished MX. Third, the valve: a shutoff valve for a single supply, a mixing or thermostatic valve for hot water. Fourth, the details for daily use, such as a hand shower or a foot shower. The finishes are on the [Fontealta finishes page](/finiture).

## What should you look for in a freestanding outdoor shower?
keyword: outdoor shower

Check four things in a freestanding **outdoor shower**, Fontealta’s or any other: material, finish, valve and anchoring. For material, Fontealta’s Club, Club Large, Classy and Boss collections use AISI 316 stainless steel, which resists salt and pool chlorine better than 304. For finish, Fontealta recommends polished MX near the sea. For the valve, pick a shutoff valve for one supply or a mixing valve (.M) for hot and cold. For a hand shower, choose an .E or .ME version. On a lawn or other open ground, the [XT200 ground anchor](/modello/xt200) keeps a **garden shower** steady.
